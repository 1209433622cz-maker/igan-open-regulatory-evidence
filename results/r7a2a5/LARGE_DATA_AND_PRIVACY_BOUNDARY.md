# R7A2A5 large-data and privacy boundary

No raw BAM, genotype, individual-level expression, donor-level genotype/LD matrix or other large third-party source file is redistributed in the R7A2A5 public assets.

Public inputs remain represented by accession, source URL, byte count and checksum in `data/LARGE_DATA_MANIFEST.tsv` and Supplementary Table S1. The supplementary workbook contains aggregate results and the frozen donor-level target-panel summaries needed to audit the reported analysis. It does not contain author identities or unpublished institutional declarations.

The manuscript and cover-letter source retain explicit placeholders for authorship, affiliations, correspondence, CRediT, funding, competing interests and local ethics/waiver wording. These fields must be completed outside the public repository with verified author-provided information before final submission.
