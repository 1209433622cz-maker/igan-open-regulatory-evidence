## 4. 根因复现

旧 schema v1.1：

```text

has_xf = 1,474,475
xf8 = 25
CB/GN/UB/unambiguous GN among xf8 = 1.0/1.0/1.0/1.0
status = FAIL because xf8 < 1,000
```
