# R7A1C frozen protocol: TenK replication and PBC liver gate v1

Date: 2026-09-15

The only eligible genes are `IL12RB2` and `FCRL3`, which passed the R7A1B signal-specific OneK gate. INAVA and all untested PBC genes are excluded from rescue.

TenK replication must use full 100 kb eQTL rows and author source-SuSiE credible sets, unique GRCh37→GRCh38 mapping, allele/rsID checks and the pre-specified compatible-cell map recorded in the R7A1B1 next-stage report. Gene-level QTL significance alone is insufficient.

The liver gate uses HRA008003. Donors are the statistical unit. Detectability requires IL12RB2 or FCRL3 nonzero pseudobulk in a predeclared liver immune lineage in at least 3 of 5 PBC donors. Case-control differences are supportive and cannot be computed with cells as independent replicates.

Advance only if at least one of two genes has compatible-cell TenK signal replication and the liver target-detectability gate passes. Otherwise freeze the PBC regulatory-main route under the predefined failure reason; do not auto-resume CeD.
