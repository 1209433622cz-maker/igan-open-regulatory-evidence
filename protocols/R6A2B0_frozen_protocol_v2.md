# R6A2B0 frozen protocol — 8-locus bounded positive-yield expansion

## Loci
TNFSF4/18, REL, TNFSF8/15, REEP3, OVOL1/RELA, TNFSF12/13, TNFRSF13B, LIF/OSM.

## Frozen universe
Exactly 183 locus×OneK1K-cell×gene combinations from the R6A2A1 release.

## Disease
combined IgAN primary; European-only and Asian-only supportive.

## Prohibitions
- no post-hoc gene/cell expansion
- no TenK disease-coloc look-ahead before OneK signal gate
- no batch source-LD
- no tissue-based gene selection
- no counting multiple cells/genes within one locus as multiple positive loci

## Exit
- >=2 additional robust/signal-specific loci -> GO full-24
- exactly 1 -> conditional one-more bounded block
- ambiguity only -> targeted source-LD
- zero interpretable positives under adequate coverage -> HOLD full-24 and reassess design
