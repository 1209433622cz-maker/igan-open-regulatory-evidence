# R7A0 sources checked — 2026-09-15

## PBC

- Gjoka & Cordell fine-mapping (2024/2025 issue):
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11656035/
  - European PBC N=24,510: 8,021 cases, 16,489 controls.
  - 56 of 57 GWS loci analysed after excluding HLA.
  - Study-derived LD matrices built from individual-level European samples.
  - Data availability states summary statistics and LD matrices are public.

- Newcastle data page:
  https://www.staff.ncl.ac.uk/heather.cordell/GjokaPaper.html
  - Public `GJOKA_SUMSTATS.zip` link.

- PBC GWAS direct public file:
  https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90061001-GCST90062000/GCST90061440/GCST90061440_buildGRCh37.tsv

- Cordell et al. 2021 PBC international GWMA:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8811537/
  - 57 GWS loci / 21 novel.
  - moloc reported 251 colocalization models at 60 loci; 28 GWS risk loci included IL12RB2, FCRL3 and INAVA examples.

- PBC liver single-cell:
  https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa-human/browse/HRA008003
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53104-9
  - HRA008003 marked Open access.
  - Published liver scRNA: 5 treatment-naive PBC + 5 hepatic-hemangioma liver controls.

- 2026 hostile-overlap examples:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41540-026-00817-w
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41819524/

## CeD

- Direct public CeD summary-statistics source:
  https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90014001-GCST90015000/GCST90014442/

- Dense Immunochip genetics:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3242065/
  - 12,041 cases + 12,228 controls.
  - 39 non-HLA risk loci / 40 including HLA; later descriptions report 57 independent non-HLA association signals.

- 2020 CeD meta-analysis:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7028987/
  - 12,948 cases + 14,826 controls; 127,855 post-QC Immunochip variants; adds two GWS loci.

- 2020 eQTL/gene-prioritization study:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7868554/
  - 118 prioritized genes across 50 CeD regions.
  - CSK supported by all four methods; TRAFD1 and UBASH3A among genes with three lines of evidence.

- 2026 duodenal scRNA:
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE315138
  - 4 active CeD + 2 normal duodenal biopsies.
